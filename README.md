# cit4350-GroupProject
Group Project for Database Programming class

Topic Selection


Our group chose SDG #3, Good Health and Well-Being because health is important for college students. 
Between classes, assignments, and other responsibilities, students may have trouble finding time to exercise, get enough 
sleep, and take care of themselves. These habits can affect their health and how they feel each day. Our project focuses on exercise, sleep, and 
self-care to better understand how they relate to students overall well being.

Project Definition Data


Our group will use six tables to organize the information. The Student table will have basic student information and their overall well-being rating. 
The Exercise Typess and Student Exercise Records tables will show what exercises students do, how often 
they exercise, and how long each session lasts. The Self Care Types and Self Care Log 
tables will keep track of self-care activities. The Sleep Log table will show how much sleep students get 
during school nights, weekends, and exam periods. We are using separate tables so we can keep the information organized, connect 
it to the correct students, and avoid repeating the same information.

Our database will help us answer three questions: What types of exercise are most common among college students? 
Do students who get more sleep report better overall well-being? What self-care activities are most common among students? 
We will use SQL queries to compare the information and look for patterns.


Project Definition Overall


The database project that is defined in this repository will focus on college student health data. This project will disperse an anonymous survey to college 
students that will collect data based on the answers to the various questions. It will also collect data from various fitness apps, such as the run keeper app,
and from sleep tracking apps, such as the SleepCycle app, to augment the survey data. We will use this data to answer questions to help address the Good Health 
and Well Being SDG. We will create queries using DDL to pull data from the database to form answers to these questions.
